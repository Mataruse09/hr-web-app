Acceptable Use Policy

By using MatinexHR, you agree to the following guidelines:

  • Legal Use: Use the platform only for lawful HR management purposes
  • No Abuse: Do not harass, threaten, or abuse other users
  • No Spam: Do not send unsolicited communications through our platform
  • Data Integrity: Do not attempt to hack or compromise our systems
  • Content Standards: Do not upload illegal, harmful, or offensive content
Violation Consequences

Violations may result in warning, suspension, or permanent ban depending on severity.

Refund Policy

  • No Refunds: Subscription fees are non-refundable
  • Trial Period: New users can use the free tier to evaluate the service
  • Cancellation: You may cancel at any time, access continues until end of billing period
  • Service Credits: In case of service downtime, we may provide credits at our discretion

Security Policy

  • Data Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control for all users
  • Regular Audits: We conduct regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Incident Response: We have procedures for handling security incidents
  • Data Backup: Regular backups to prevent data loss

Support Policy

  • Response Time: We aim to respond to all support requests within 24 hours
  • Support Channels: Email support and in-app ticketing system
  • Knowledge Base: Self-service help articles and documentation
  • Priority Support: Enterprise customers receive priority support
