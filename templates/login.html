How to Use WorkZen HR
  1. Select your company from the dropdown menu
  2. Enter your username provided by your administrator
  3. Enter your password and click Sign In
  4. Navigate modules using the sidebar: Employees, Attendance, Leave, Payroll, Analytics, Performance, AI Predictions
  5. Check your dashboard for insights, notifications, and quick actions
  6. Contact support if you need help - click the Need Help button
