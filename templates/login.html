How to Use WorkZen HR

Important: If your company is not registered yet, please click "Register your company" below the login form first!

  1. Select your company from the dropdown menu (must be registered first)
  2. Register if needed: Click "Register your company" link below the login form
  3. Enter your username provided by your administrator
  4. Enter your password and click Sign In
  5. Navigate modules using the sidebar: Employees, Attendance, Leave, Payroll, Analytics, Performance, AI Predictions
  6. Check your dashboard for insights, notifications, and quick actions
  7. Contact support if you need help - click the Need Help button
